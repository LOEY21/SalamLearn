Sand-sprinkle trace effect

Every few px of finger movement spawns 2-4 sand grains at the touch point:

• vx: small random horizontal drift (±40px/s)
• vy: starts near 0, gravity pulls down (~90px/s²)
• size 2-4px, color sampled from 3 sand tones (light/mid/dark tan) + rare gold glint (10%)
• rotation drifts slowly for a tumbling look
• fades out over 550-750ms (opacity + slight scale-down), not linear — ease-in on the fade
• grains land ~14-22px below the touch point on average, matching how real sand kicks up and settles just under/behind a dragging finger

This is ambient/secondary motion only — ink stroke itself (ported separately) is the primary layer; sand is texture/feedback, never blocks legibility of the guide letter underneath.
