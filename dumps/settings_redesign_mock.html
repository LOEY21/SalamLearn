What changed vs. current screen

1. Identity strip added — current screen never says whose settings these are or that the parent-PIN gate succeeded. New mint strip up top answers both in one glance.

2. Sliders consolidated into one card — was already one SoftCard, kept, just tightened row dividers instead of loose SizedBox gaps so it reads as one control group, not three floating widgets.

3. Email / Sync / Switch account merged into one "Account & Data" list — currently three separate same-weight cards (mint sync card, plain switch card, plus email card) competing for attention. Collapsing into one bordered list with icon+title+sub+action per row matches the density of a real settings screen and cuts vertical scroll roughly in half.

4. Erase moved to a distinct "danger zone" block — currently a SoftCard tinted coral, same shape/elevation as every other card, easy to misjudge as equally safe to tap. Dashed coral border + tint + explicit "DANGER ZONE" eyebrow signals irreversibility the way the current card doesn't.

5. Dropped the big teal gradient hero banner — was pure decoration (icon + "App Settings" + generic subtitle), ~110px of vertical space for zero information. Replaced with the compact identity strip which carries real information instead.
