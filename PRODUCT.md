# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Three roles share one app binary:
- **Learners (ages 5–11, Grade 1)**: Muslim children following the Philippine Refined Elementary Madrasah Curriculum (ALIVE). They use the Student Hub on a phone or tablet, often a parent's device, at home or in class. Many are still early readers, so they rely on pictures, icons, the mascot and audio more than on text.
- **Parents/Guardians**: behind a PIN. They check progress, manage the child's profile and consent, and get "try at home" guidance.
- **Asatidz (teachers)**: behind a PIN. They run a class dashboard and cast games to a classroom screen (Hot Seat, choral recitation).

## Product Purpose
SalamLearn is an offline-first Islamic education app. It teaches Arabic letters, greetings, Qur'an etiquette, Sirah, Fiqh basics and values through short games laid out along an illustrated Adventure Map. Success means a child comes back every day and finishes stages, and the adults can see that learning happened. It is a BSIT capstone project. The visual spec comes from the REFERENCE mockup documents.

## Brand Personality
Warm, adventurous and reverent. For learners it should feel like a storybook game world: an illustrated map, glowing checkpoints, a friendly mascot and a sense of collecting things. For adults it should feel calm, trustworthy and school-grade. Bilingual: English and Filipino (Tagalog), with Arabic shown for religious content.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (white cards, gradient hero metrics) on child-facing screens.
- Loud mobile-game monetisation patterns: loot boxes, fake currencies, leaderboards, pressure timers.
- Fake data. No hardcoded levels, XP rings or stats that don't come from real progress.
- Anything that trivialises religious content.

## Design Principles
1. **One world for the child.** Every learner screen should feel like part of the Adventure Map's illustrated world, not a separate app.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Pictures, icons and the mascot come first. Keep text short enough for a 6-year-old (or a parent reading aloud).
3. **Honest progress.** Every number shown to a child or parent comes from real data.
4. **Earned delight.** Motion and celebration mark real achievements. They are not decoration on every surface.
5. **Grown-up doors stay closed.** Settings, account switching and data controls are always behind the parent/teacher PIN.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Large tap targets (at least 48dp) for small hands, with no precision gestures needed for core actions.
- Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 for body copy, including on illustrated or tinted backgrounds.
- All UI copy must work in Filipino. Translated strings run 20–40% longer than English, so layouts must wrap rather than clip.
- Respect reduced-motion settings for looping ambient animation.
